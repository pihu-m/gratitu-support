Frequently Asked Questions

How does Gratitu work?

Set an alarm like you normally would. When it goes off, you'll be asked to say or type one thing you're grateful for. The app uses Apple Intelligence (on-device) to verify your response is genuine gratitude — then your alarm is dismissed and your entry is saved.

What if I can't think of something to say?

It can be anything, even something small — "I'm grateful for my morning coffee" or "I'm grateful I slept well." The app isn't looking for perfection, just a genuine expression of gratitude.

Can I type instead of speaking?

Yes. You can switch between voice and text input. If speech recognition isn't available or you'd prefer to type, the app will offer text input as an alternative.

What is the grace window?

The grace window is the amount of time you have to capture your gratitude before the alarm re-triggers. You can adjust this in Settings. If you don't respond in time, the alarm will sound again to give you another chance.

Does Gratitu require Apple Intelligence?

Gratitu works best with Apple Intelligence enabled for gratitude verification. On devices where it's not available, the app will still accept your gratitude responses. You can check your Apple Intelligence status in the app's Settings.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your data — alarms, gratitude entries, and preferences — stays on your device. Speech recognition and gratitude verification happen entirely on-device. Nothing is sent to any server.

What do I get with the free version?

The free version includes one alarm and a 7-day trial of all premium features. After the trial, you can continue using one alarm for free or upgrade for unlimited alarms, full streak history, and all future features.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone to view, change, or cancel your Gratitu subscription. You can also manage subscriptions in the App Store app.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Gratitu, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Your premium access will be restored if you have an active subscription or lifetime purchase on your Apple ID.

The app isn't recognizing my speech. What should I do?

  1. Make sure Gratitu has microphone permission — go to Settings > Gratitu on your iPhone and enable Microphone.
  2. Speak clearly in a quiet environment.
  3. If speech recognition isn't working, try switching to text input mode.
  4. If problems persist, contact us at pihumventures@gmail.com.

My alarm didn't go off. What happened?

Gratitu uses AlarmKit to schedule alarms. Make sure notifications are enabled for Gratitu in your iPhone Settings, and that your device is not in a Focus mode that silences Gratitu. If the issue persists, try deleting and re-creating the alarm.

What iOS version do I need?

Gratitu requires iOS 26 or later.
